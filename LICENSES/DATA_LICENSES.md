The GBD-derived processed files remain subject to the terms accepted when the source data were obtained, including the IHME Free-of-Charge Non-Commercial User Agreement and applicable citation requirements.

Suggested source citation:

## Natural Earth map data

Map geometries are accessed through the R package `rnaturalearth`. Natural Earth states that its vector and raster map data are in the public domain. Users should consult the current Natural Earth terms for details: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
